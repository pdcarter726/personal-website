Hi, I'm Peter. I'm a senior computer science student at NC State University graduating in December 2026. I started out in Java, Spring Boot and Hibernate, then moved into TypeScript and React, and since August 2025 I've been part of Neurotech at NC State, where I now lead the software team and work with EEG data. If you want to see what I've built, head over to my work page.

Outside of code, I’m happiest when I’m traveling, cooking, or hunting down something new to eat. Music is a big part of my life too: my playlists run from pop all the way to metal, and I play piano. I also like staying active, and the gym is a regular part of my week. Looking ahead, I’d love to visit as many national parks as I can and, eventually, train for and finish an Ironman.
